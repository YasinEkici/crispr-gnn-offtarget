# The Impact of Chromatin Dynamics on Cas9-Mediated Genome Editing in Human Cells

## Citation

Daer, R. M., Cutts, J. P., Brafman, D. A., & Haynes, K. A. (2017). The Impact of Chromatin Dynamics on Cas9-Mediated Genome Editing in Human Cells. *ACS Synthetic Biology*, 6(3), 428-438. https://doi.org/10.1021/acssynbio.5b00299

## Core Idea

This study shows that closed chromatin can inhibit Cas9-mediated editing in human cells and that chromatin state is an important contextual factor for genome editing outcomes.

## Project Relevance

It supports the use of chromatin accessibility and epigenetic context as biologically meaningful predictors rather than arbitrary extra covariates. This is relevant to Sprint 7E's finding that masking experimental epigenetic target-observation features collapses performance.

## Claim Boundary

Use as biological motivation. The local model remains predictive; it does not experimentally manipulate chromatin.
