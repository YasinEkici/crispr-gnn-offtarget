# CHANGE-seq reveals genetic and epigenetic effects on CRISPR-Cas9 genome-wide activity

## Citation

Lazzarotto, C. R., Malinin, N. L., Li, Y., et al. (2020). CHANGE-seq reveals genetic and epigenetic effects on CRISPR-Cas9 genome-wide activity. *Nature Biotechnology*, 38(11), 1317-1327. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-020-0555-7

## Core Idea

CHANGE-seq is a high-throughput method for profiling CRISPR-Cas9 genome-wide activity. The study connects off-target activity with genetic and epigenetic context, including chromatin accessibility and regulatory annotations.

## Project Relevance

This paper supports the biological plausibility of Sprint 7E/7F findings that target-observation and experimental epigenetic context features carry important predictive signal. It is not a direct dataset match, but it strengthens the rationale for chromatin/context-aware CRISPR off-target prediction.

## Claim Boundary

Use this as biological motivation for epigenetic context features. Do not use it to claim that any specific Sprint 7/8 feature is causal in the local measured-only benchmark.
